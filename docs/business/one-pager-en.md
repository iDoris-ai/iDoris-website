### How we work — four stages

| Stage | What happens | Typical output |
|---|---|---|
| **Discover** | 3–5 days on site. We watch how you actually work, measure it, and find the highest-value opportunities. | Workflow map · AI readiness score · **annualised hours per pain point** · Top 3 use cases · working prototype · 90-day roadmap |
| **Enable** | We teach your staff to use AI **inside the work they already do** — not how to use a chatbot. | Role-specific playbook · reusable Skill Pack |
| **Implement** | Skills, agents, or integrations — scoped to what you're actually ready for. | Working system · UAT · manuals · before/after measurement |
| **Operate** | Models, prices and workflows keep changing. We keep it working. | Monitoring · cost control · monthly optimisation |

**You can stop after any stage.** Many clients do Discovery and implement it themselves.
That is a successful outcome.
