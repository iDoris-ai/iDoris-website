The problem

Most organizations don't need another AI presentation. They need to know which of their everyday tasks are worth handing to AI — and which are not.

You probably don't know how many hours a year your team spends on repetitive work. Almost nobody does. That number is the first thing we give you.

What we will tell you that others won't

  • If your organisation isn't ready for AI yet, we will say so — in writing, in the deliverables you paid for.
  • If a free government or university course covers what you need, go take it. We can't compete with free, and we won't pretend to.
  • We default to human approval on every AI output that reaches your customers. Full automation is something you opt into, in writing, after the data supports it.
