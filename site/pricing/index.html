Where this argument stops

  • Not every Chinese model is cheap. Kimi K3 lists at $3 in / $15 out — level with Claude Sonnet 5. The saving comes from picking the right model for the job, not from the flag on the vendor. We route by task, not by flag.
  • Capability is not identical. Depending on the task — coding, reasoning, image, video — expect roughly 80–90% of the frontier US models. For a great deal of real work that is the whole job; for the hardest 10% it is not, and we will say so rather than sell you the wrong thing.
  • Caching cuts both sides — unevenly. A cached input token costs $0.50 on Claude Opus 5 and $0.0036 on DeepSeek V4 Pro. On cache-heavy agent workloads the gap gets wider, not narrower.
  • Prices move. DeepSeek has publicly signalled an increase; Chinese labs cut rates repeatedly through the first half of 2026. Everything here is dated 8 August 2026, and we re-check it before quoting.
  • Sources. Every figure is taken from the vendor’s own official pricing documentation — Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, DeepSeek, Z.ai (Zhipu), Moonshot (Kimi) and Alibaba Cloud Model Studio. No third-party aggregator numbers appear on this page.
